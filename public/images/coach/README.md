# Coach photos

Put your pictures in this folder, then list them in `server/coach.js` under
`photos` and set `publish: true`.

```js
photos: [
  { src: '/images/coach/pitching.jpg', alt: 'Bubba Hall delivering a pitch from the mound' },
  { src: '/images/coach/lesson.jpg',   alt: 'Bubba Hall working with a young hitter in the cage' },
],
```

## Use photos you own

Use pictures you took, that your family took, or that you have permission to
use. Do **not** save images off a college athletics site, MaxPreps, Perfect
Game, or a news article — those belong to the photographer or the school, and
this site takes payments, which is the use they act on.

If a school or photographer shot a picture you like, email and ask. They often
say yes for a former player, and then you have it in writing.

## What works well

- **Landscape, roughly 3:2.** Around 1600px wide is plenty; the page scales it.
- **Action first.** One good shot mid-delivery beats three posed ones.
- **One coaching photo** — you working with a kid — does more for bookings than
  any playing photo, because it shows parents what they're buying.
- **Keep files under ~500KB** so the page stays fast on a phone. Export as JPEG
  at around 80% quality.

## Photos of minors

If another player is recognizable in a shot, get their parent's OK before it
goes on a page selling lessons. A photo taken from behind, or wide enough that
faces aren't clear, avoids the question.
